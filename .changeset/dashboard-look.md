The dashboard has a look of its own instead of the browser's defaults: a top bar that marks the current section, pages with a set width, tables, forms and buttons styled alike, messages in colored boxes, and a dark theme that follows the system. A project's pages (runs, environment, audit log, settings) are tabs under its name, its components are chips, pulling a ref is one row, and the project form puts short fields side by side.
